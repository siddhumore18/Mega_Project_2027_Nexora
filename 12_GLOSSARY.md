# Glossary

- **Exam cycle:** a named examination period with configuration and workflow status.
- **Exam offering:** a particular subject/paper being examined during a cycle for a defined cohort.
- **Eligibility:** a policy-driven decision about whether a student may register for an offering, with reasons.
- **Backlog:** an outstanding/repeat subject or attempt, as defined by institutional policy.
- **Hard constraint:** a mandatory rule that a published schedule must satisfy.
- **Soft constraint:** a preference that the optimizer tries to satisfy while respecting hard constraints.
- **Candidate schedule:** a generated draft that has not been approved or published.
- **Schedule version:** immutable snapshot of a schedule at a point in the workflow.
- **Infeasible:** the solver determined that no solution satisfies the modeled constraints for the supplied problem.
- **Unknown/timeout:** the solver did not establish feasibility or infeasibility within the available run.
- **Independent validation:** a separate validation pass that checks the candidate against hard rules before approval/publication.
- **Input snapshot:** versioned record of the data and policy settings used for a scheduling run.
